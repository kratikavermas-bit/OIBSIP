# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to understand sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and other important business trends.

The analysis uses Python and popular data analysis and visualization libraries to extract meaningful insights from the dataset.

## 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand the structure and characteristics of the retail sales dataset.
- Perform initial data inspection and preprocessing.
- Calculate descriptive statistics for numerical variables.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics such as age groups and gender.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
- Analyze revenue generated by different product categories.
- Understand relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Create meaningful visualizations to identify hidden patterns.
- Provide actionable business recommendations based on the findings.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Inspection

The dataset was loaded and inspected to understand:

- Number of rows and columns
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing values
- Basic structure of the dataset

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics were calculated for numerical columns, including:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation
- Minimum and maximum values

### 3. Time Series Analysis

Sales trends were analyzed over time using:

- Monthly sales trends
- Quarterly sales trends
- Line charts

This helps identify changes in sales performance over different periods.

### 4. Customer Demographics

Customer behaviour was explored through:

- Age group distribution
- Gender distribution
- Comparison of purchasing behaviour across demographic groups

### 5. Product Analysis

Product performance was analyzed by:

- Identifying the top 10 best-selling products
- Comparing revenue across product categories
- Creating bar charts for product performance

### 6. Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix and heatmap were created to understand relationships between numerical variables in the dataset.

### 7. Additional Visualization

An additional visualization was created to identify a non-obvious pattern or insight from the retail sales data.

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes visualizations such as:

- Monthly sales trend
- Quarterly sales trend
- Age group distribution
- Gender distribution
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Additional business-focused visualization

Observations and interpretations are provided after the charts in the notebook.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Periods of high and low sales.
- Customer demographic patterns.
- Best-performing products.
- High-revenue product categories.
- Relationships between important numerical variables.
- Potential areas for improving sales and marketing strategies.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

1. Focus marketing campaigns on the customer groups showing stronger purchasing behaviour.
2. Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products and categories.
3. Use monthly and quarterly sales trends to plan promotions and inventory requirements.
4. Develop targeted marketing strategies based on customer demographics.
5. Use data-driven insights to improve product and sales strategies.
